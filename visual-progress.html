The working scope

A browser-native reconstruction, continuing the existing app. The working scope is Mario, Link, Kirby, Pikachu and Final Destination, with training and local matches.

A larger roster, more maps, story mode and online play are outside this initial scope.

The original models, animations and attack data are in use. Movement, knockback, shields, capture, projectile travel, bomb fuse, Stone armor and special-move transitions are still browser reconstructions that need refinement. Rendering and effects are approximate; the backdrop is static.

Floor tech timing and common knockdown rules remain reconstructed. Wall and ceiling techs, full sound and effects, and stronger CPU play remain unfinished. Link’s shield coverage and rebound response still need fidelity tuning. Dodge air movement and landing rules remain reconstructed. The current build is playable but does not yet reproduce all of Brawl’s behavior.
