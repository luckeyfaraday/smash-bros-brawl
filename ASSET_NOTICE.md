Brawl Browser Lab is an independent fan and research project. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nintendo or the Super Smash Bros.
brand. Game names, characters and other third-party material remain the property
of their respective rights holders.

The [MIT license](LICENSE) covers original project code and documentation whose
copyright is held by the project's contributors. It does not grant rights to
game models, maps, textures, artwork, animation, audio, logos, extracted scripts
or other third-party material. Those materials retain their owners' rights and
applicable terms.

The playable exports in `public/assets/` and disc images are not included in Git.
The repository does contain game-derived AI/stage data under `src/data/`,
captures under `public/progress/`, and research references in the documentation.
None of that game-derived material is relicensed under MIT by this project.

Fonts and software references also have separate licenses. In particular, the
OpenSA3 reference tables retain GPLv3 terms. See
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for the individual files,
attribution and license texts.

Public availability is not a grant of rights to reuse or host third-party
assets. Determine the applicable permissions before distributing those materials.
When sharing the original MIT-licensed code, preserve its copyright and
permission notice.

Rights holders can request review or removal through the contact options on
[the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/luckeyfaraday). If a private
channel is needed, follow the contact process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
